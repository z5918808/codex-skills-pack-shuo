# Delight

Use the project design context from `impeccable`. Delight is optional and comes after task clarity.

## Decision Rule

Add delight only at a natural emotional moment: meaningful success, discovery, onboarding, progress, or a reversible low-stress interaction.

- Match brand, culture, frequency, and the user’s mental state.
- Keep the core action immediate and the flourish skippable or ignorable.
- Prefer product-specific copy or behavior over confetti, particles, easter eggs, and generic jokes.
- Sound and haptics require context, platform support, and user control.
- Repeated use must not become slower or annoying.
- Preserve reduced motion, screen-reader meaning, and performance.

Do not add playfulness to critical errors, money, safety, privacy, or urgent operations unless the product explicitly calls for it.

Verify first use and hundredth use. Remove the effect if users notice it more than the accomplishment.
