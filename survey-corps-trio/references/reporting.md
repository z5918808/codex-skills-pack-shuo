# Return delivery

Canonical routing, consultation and continuation rules. Load before dispatch, on resume and before an assignment-ending final; reuse valid same-version context.

## Route and useful packets

Levi returns results/errors to Hange once per coherent segment or blocking exception. Hange reviews and dispatches routine successors without Erwin approval. Consult Erwin early when a concrete judgment can reduce trial-and-error, resolve important uncertainty or improve the approach; also escalate changed framework/authority, repeated unresolved gaps, blockers and whole-goal acceptance. Do not forward every batch or avoid useful consultation merely to save tokens.

Bind actual destination chat/host and callable direct-message tool, task/run/generation/assignment, rules/hash and receipt location. Never infer IDs from titles; missing return capability blocks dispatch. Omit model/thinking overrides on return calls, or use receiver contract settings only when required by the tool.

Send compact criterion coverage, changed artifact references/hashes, decisive evidence, unresolved gaps and owned-action state. Preserve required per-item proof in artifacts. Results end in the same message with「依整體目標與目前進度，我下一步應完成哪個具體成果？」; consultations ask only their concrete decision question, with relevant evidence and failed approaches when any. No separate nudge or acknowledgement-only turn. Separately authorized clones return through their original parent: Levi clones -> Levi -> Hange; Hange clones -> Hange -> Erwin when consultation/escalation/closure is due. No bypass or cloned authority.

## Consultation is not ownership transfer

Bind question event_id, assignment/generation and contract hash; pause only answer-dependent work. Existing independent Levi work continues within its brief. Arrange ready independent work at normal handoffs when feasible, without delaying useful consultation or sending after a required yield. Hange's own work may await its next actual entry.

Before advice or final judgment, Erwin checks current contract version/hash, whole-goal outcome and remaining gaps; reuse valid same-version context, refresh only stale/missing details. Reply to the original question and version with unchanged direction, guidance or a required contract change. Hange validates stop/assignment state, consumes the reply and continues within authority without another acknowledgement. Consultation does not terminate/reassign work; stale advice cannot revive stopped work, rewrite a pinned brief or duplicate dispatch. Actual contract/ownership changes follow the main skill lifecycle.

## Deliver once

1. Preserve the compact packet in the existing receipt before sending: stable event_id, sender/destination, assignment/generation, status, evidence references, unresolved findings, owned-action state and next_action_needed. Messaging revocation takes precedence.
2. Use the bound direct-message tool; record explicit success identity or locatable tool result, then yield. Local final text is not delivery; sent does not prove consumption or wake-up.
3. Definite correctable rejection allows one corrected retry. Ambiguous timeout remains unknown: resend only with supported idempotency or proof of non-delivery; event_id alone is insufficient.
4. Failed recovery ends visibly as delivery_failed with redacted exact error, destination/event/assignment and missing recovery action. At next authorized entry, reconcile and recover unfinished sends only, never confirmed sends or completed work.

## Consume and continue

Validate identity, generation, role, evidence and stop state. Before effects, record consumed event_id plus intended next action/assignment as pending in the existing receipt. Complete continuation only with actual dispatch receipt, verified local result, exact blocker or accepted completion. After interruption, reconcile actual effects and authority, then finish pending work: consumed input alone does not mean its continuation completed. Duplicate input cannot repeat effects; unknown sends use the delivery rule above. No new queue or scheduler.

Before Hange voluntarily ends on an unfinished goal, require either a valid dispatched successor or a successfully sent consultation/result awaiting Erwin. Otherwise continue eligible work or actually send Erwin the result/blocker, evidence, remaining gap and applicable question, verifying delivery before ending. A local review, segment pass, planned dispatch or unsent packet is insufficient. Complete pending returns before voluntary final; forced interruption requires reconciliation. Stop/messaging revocation and accepted whole-goal closure override continuation; failed/unknown delivery follows bounded recovery, not forced resend.

Erwin continues retained work and yields after delegation; Hange/Levi yield after successful dispatch/return. No cross-chat polling, wait_threads, heartbeat, watchers, artificial wake-ups, shadow execution or reporting-only turns. Bounded waits for an owner's synchronous action require owned-process reconciliation. Use meaningful verified milestones; report stoppage, material anomalies, required decisions and completion promptly, and answer status requests from current evidence. Follow requested cadence and active-turn communication requirements; tool messages are app-visible.
