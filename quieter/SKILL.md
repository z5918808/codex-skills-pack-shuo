# Quieter

Use the project design context from `impeccable`; preserve the core identity while reducing competing signals.

1. Identify the real intensity source: saturation, contrast, scale, density, effects, or motion.
2. Keep one or two anchors and let secondary elements recede.
3. Remove decorative layers before weakening useful hierarchy.
4. Reduce motion distance, frequency, or flourish while preserving state feedback.
5. Keep affordances, focus, status, and danger signals distinct.

Quiet does not mean gray, tiny, low contrast, evenly weighted, or personality-free. Do not desaturate semantic warnings or make functional controls visually disappear.

Verify extended reading, high-stress tasks, contrast, focus, reduced motion, and whether the primary action remains obvious.
