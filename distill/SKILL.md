# Distill

Use the project design context from `impeccable`; simplify the user’s path, not the domain’s truth.

1. Name the primary user decision or action.
2. Inventory elements by required, supporting, conditional, redundant, or decorative.
3. Delete redundancy first; then combine, reorder, or progressively disclose.
4. Keep information needed for safe or informed decisions visible at the right moment.
5. Reduce visual treatments and variants only after preserving hierarchy and accessibility.
6. Prefer a shorter task path over a visually emptier screen.

Do not hide complexity inside mystery controls, remove rare but critical paths, or apply one-primary-action dogma to genuine multi-action tools.

Verify task completion, error recovery, expert workflows, empty and dense states, and the before/after number of decisions or steps.
